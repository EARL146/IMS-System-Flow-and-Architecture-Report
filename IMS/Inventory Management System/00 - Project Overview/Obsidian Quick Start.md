# Open the IMS notes in Obsidian

This `IMS` folder is the project's Obsidian vault. Open the project `IMS/` folder in Obsidian and then open [[../Inventory Management Home]]. Project notes are grouped under `Inventory Management System/`.

The vault's `.obsidian/` settings remain local to this machine and are excluded from Git. The repository's canonical copy of these notes is under `docs/obsidian/Inventory Management System/`.
